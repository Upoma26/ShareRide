How it works

1

Sign up and verify

Register with your phone, confirm the OTP and upload your NID. An admin checks it before you can ride.

2

Post or find a ride

Drivers post their route, time, seats and fare. Passengers search by route and date and request seats.

3

Travel and pay

Show your Trip PIN when you get in, ride together, then pay by cash, bKash, Nagad or card and rate each other.
