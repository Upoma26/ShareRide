About ShareRide

A community ride-sharing platform for safe and affordable travel in Dhaka.

How it works

1

Sign up and verify

Register with your phone, confirm the OTP and upload your NID. An admin checks it.

2

Post or find a ride

Drivers post route, time, seats and fare. Passengers search and request seats.

3

Travel and pay

Show your Trip PIN, ride together, pay your way and rate each other.

Features

🪪

NID verified members

Every driver and passenger is checked by an admin.

🔢

Trip PIN check-in

A one-time PIN proves the right passenger got in.

🚨

One-tap SOS

Send your live location to admins during a trip.

💬

Ride group chat

Talk to your driver and co-passengers. Closes 24 hours after the trip.

⭐

Two-way ratings

Drivers and passengers rate each other after every trip.

💳

Pay your way

Cash, bKash, Nagad or card, with a printable receipt.

Your safety

  • Driver plate and phone are shared only after your booking is accepted.
  • Never share your Trip PIN with anyone except your driver at pickup.
  • Use the SOS button or call 999 if you feel unsafe.
  • Report any problem from your booking page. Admins review every complaint.
